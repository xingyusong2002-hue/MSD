Welcome to Chromatic Echoes

A few seconds of context before you step inside

You are about to enter the Dead Room — an anechoic chamber that absorbs almost all sound. Your voice will sound flat. Your claps will not echo. Even silence will feel different.

Inside, you will become a colour. When you make sound, the room will paint with it on the screen at the centre. Working with the others, you'll learn what loudness, rhythm, and silence look like.
